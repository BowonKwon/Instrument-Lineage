Lineages of instruments

A site that follows where instruments came from and how they changed and carried on, through documents, records and pictures. Three chapters are planned; chapter 3 is published first. It has been checked independently but is still an exploratory edition.

Chapters#

  • 3 · Akhak gwebeom: a 1493 picture book of instruments, and what came after · exploratory edition 0.2
    How the 64 pictured entries (58 instruments, 6 ritual objects) are used today, how the book was printed again and again, and the gap between when a document set something down and when an instrument was used.
  • 1 · Patents and invention · in preparation
    Instrument patents in nineteenth-century Britain, France and the United States.
  • 2 · Orchestration · in preparation
    When instruments entered and left the scores of symphonies.
